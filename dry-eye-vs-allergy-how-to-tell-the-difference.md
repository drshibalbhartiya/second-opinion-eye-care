# Dry Eye vs Allergy: How Do You Tell the Difference?

Many patients experience:

- Burning  
- Watering  
- Redness  

And wonder:
👉 Is this dryness or allergy?

---

## Why this gets confusing

Both conditions affect the eye surface  
Both can feel uncomfortable  

But they are very different.

---

## Dry eye typically causes

- Burning or gritty feeling  
- Fluctuating vision  
- Worse with screen use  
- Relief after blinking  

---

## Allergy typically causes

- Itching (very common)  
- Redness  
- Swelling  
- Seasonal pattern  

---

## Key difference

👉 **Itching = more likely allergy**  
👉 **Burning + fluctuation = more likely dry eye**

---

## Why it matters

Treatment is different.

Using the wrong treatment can:
👉 delay relief  

---

## The core idea

**Not all irritation is the same—correct diagnosis matters.**
