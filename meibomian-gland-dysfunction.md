# How to Improve Meibomian Gland Function

> **Healthy meibomian glands are essential for comfortable, clear vision. They produce the oily layer of your tears that prevents tears from evaporating too quickly.**

Many people with dry eye disease have perfectly normal tear production.

The problem is that their tears evaporate too quickly because the meibomian glands are not working properly.

Fortunately, meibomian gland dysfunction (MGD) can often be improved with the right treatment and daily habits.

---

# What Are the Meibomian Glands?

The meibomian glands are tiny oil-producing glands located inside the upper and lower eyelids.

Every time you blink, they release a thin layer of oil (called **meibum**) onto the surface of your tears.

This oil helps:

- Reduce tear evaporation
- Keep the tear film stable
- Improve visual quality
- Protect the surface of the eye
- Make blinking comfortable

Without enough healthy oil, tears evaporate quickly, leading to dry eye symptoms.

---

# What is Meibomian Gland Dysfunction (MGD)?

MGD occurs when these glands become blocked, inflamed, or produce poor-quality oil.

Instead of flowing easily, the oil becomes thick, sticky, or toothpaste-like.

Eventually, some glands may stop functioning altogether.

MGD is one of the most common causes of evaporative dry eye disease.

---

# Common Symptoms

You may experience:

- Dry eyes
- Burning
- Stinging
- Grittiness
- Blurred vision
- Vision that improves after blinking
- Excessive watering
- Heavy eyelids
- Eye fatigue
- Contact lens intolerance
- Red eyelid margins

Symptoms often fluctuate throughout the day.

---

# Why Does MGD Develop?

Several factors contribute.

These include:

- Ageing
- Rosacea
- Blepharitis
- Hormonal changes
- Menopause
- Long hours of screen use
- Incomplete blinking
- Contact lens wear
- Certain medications

Most patients have more than one contributing factor.

---

# Why Does Screen Use Make It Worse?

When using digital devices, we blink less often and less completely.

Incomplete blinking means the glands are not emptied properly.

Over time:

- oil stagnates
- glands become blocked
- tears evaporate faster
- symptoms worsen

Blinking fully is one of the simplest ways to improve gland function.

---

# Can Meibomian Glands Recover?

Often, yes.

Many blocked glands begin functioning better with consistent treatment.

However, glands that have been permanently lost cannot usually be regenerated.

This is why early diagnosis and treatment are important.

---

# Warm Compresses

Warm compresses are one of the most effective home treatments.

Heat softens the thickened oil inside the glands.

General tips include:

- Use a clean warm compress designed for eyelid therapy or another safe heat source.
- Apply it for about 5–10 minutes, following your eye care professional's advice.
- The compress should feel comfortably warm, not hot enough to burn the skin.
- Repeat as recommended by your ophthalmologist.

Consistency is more important than intensity.

---

# Eyelid Massage

After warming the eyelids, gentle massage may help express softened oil from the glands.

This should be done carefully and according to your ophthalmologist's instructions.

Excessive pressure can irritate the eyelids.

---

# Eyelid Hygiene

Keeping the eyelid margins clean helps reduce inflammation.

This may involve:

- Gentle lid cleansing
- Removing debris from the lash line
- Managing blepharitis
- Following your prescribed lid hygiene routine

Good eyelid hygiene supports long-term gland health.

---

# Blink Better

Many people do not blink completely.

Try to:

- Blink slowly.
- Close your eyelids fully.
- Pause briefly before opening them again.

Complete blinking helps spread oil evenly across the tear film.

---

# Take Regular Screen Breaks

During prolonged screen use:

- Follow the 20-20-20 rule.
- Blink consciously.
- Position screens slightly below eye level.
- Avoid staring continuously for long periods.

Small changes can make a noticeable difference.

---

# Artificial Tears

Lubricating eye drops often improve comfort.

For patients with evaporative dry eye, lipid-containing artificial tears may provide additional benefit by helping stabilise the tear film.

Your ophthalmologist can recommend the most appropriate formulation.

---

# Nutrition

A healthy diet supports overall eye health.

Some people may benefit from increasing dietary omega-3 fatty acids through foods such as:

- Oily fish
- Walnuts
- Flaxseed

The evidence for omega-3 supplements in dry eye disease is mixed, and they are not appropriate for everyone. Discuss supplements with your doctor, particularly if you take blood-thinning medication or have other medical conditions.

---

# Stay Hydrated

Adequate hydration supports general health and may help maintain a healthy tear film.

Drink enough water throughout the day unless you have been advised to restrict fluids for another medical reason.

---

# In-Office Treatments

If home treatment is insufficient, additional options may include:

- Meibomian gland expression
- Thermal pulsation therapy
- Intense pulsed light (IPL) for selected patients
- Treatment of associated blepharitis
- Prescription medications to reduce inflammation

Not every patient requires these treatments.

Recommendations depend on examination findings and symptom severity.

---

# What Makes MGD Worse?

Symptoms may worsen with:

- Air conditioning
- Smoke
- Dry environments
- Long hours on digital devices
- Poor sleep
- Inconsistent treatment
- Untreated rosacea

Recognising triggers helps improve long-term control.

---

# How Long Does Improvement Take?

MGD usually improves gradually.

Many people notice improvement after several weeks of consistent treatment.

Long-standing disease often requires ongoing maintenance.

---

# My Philosophy

Meibomian gland dysfunction is a chronic condition.

There is rarely a single treatment that fixes everything overnight.

Successful management combines:

- Accurate diagnosis
- Healthy eyelid habits
- Consistent home care
- Appropriate lubricants
- Treatment of inflammation when needed
- Regular follow-up

The goal is not simply to improve symptoms today.

The goal is to preserve healthy meibomian glands for years to come.

---

# Frequently Asked Questions

## Can blocked meibomian glands be opened?

Often, yes.

Many glands improve with appropriate treatment, especially when managed early.

---

## How often should I use warm compresses?

This depends on the severity of your condition and your ophthalmologist's advice.

---

## Does MGD cause blurred vision?

Yes.

An unstable tear film can cause fluctuating or blurred vision that often improves after blinking.

---

## Can MGD return?

Yes.

It is usually a chronic condition that benefits from ongoing maintenance.

---

## Is MGD the same as dry eye?

Not exactly.

MGD is one of the most common causes of evaporative dry eye disease, but dry eye can also result from reduced tear production and other conditions.

---

# Key Take-Home Messages

- Healthy meibomian glands produce the oil that keeps tears from evaporating too quickly.
- MGD is one of the leading causes of dry eye disease.
- Warm compresses, eyelid hygiene and complete blinking are the foundation of treatment.
- Early treatment helps preserve gland function.
- Most people improve with a consistent, individualised treatment plan.
