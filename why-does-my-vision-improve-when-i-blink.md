# Why Does My Vision Improve When I Blink?

Some patients notice:

“Vision becomes clearer after I blink.”

---

## What this means

Blinking refreshes the tear film.

A stable tear film:
👉 improves optical clarity  

---

## When this becomes noticeable

- During screen use  
- After prolonged focus  
- In dry environments  

---

## What it suggests

- Tear film instability  
- Early dry eye  
- Surface irregularity  

---

## Why tests may be normal

Because:
👉 structure is fine  
👉 function is affected  

---

## The core idea

**Blinking improves vision because it restores the tear surface.**
