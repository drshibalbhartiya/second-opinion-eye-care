# Why Do Patients Feel Visually Impaired Before Tests Change?

Patients often describe that something feels “off” long before routine measures appear abnormal.

They may still read the chart well.
Visual fields may remain stable.
OCT values may appear reassuring.

And yet:

- reading feels more effortful
- low light becomes uncomfortable
- driving confidence changes
- navigation feels slower
- vision feels less reliable despite appearing clear

This raises a recurring question:

Are we measuring enough of the visual experience to understand early disease?

Across glaucoma and related conditions, structural and functional adaptation may allow compensation for years before standard thresholds identify change.

The challenge is not simply detecting disease.

It is recognising when patients are noticing something before our systems do.
