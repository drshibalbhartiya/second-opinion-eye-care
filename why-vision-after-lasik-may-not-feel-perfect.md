# Why Does Vision After LASIK Not Always Feel Perfect?

Many patients expect perfect vision after LASIK.

Most achieve excellent clarity.

But some notice:
👉 vision feels different

---

## What patients describe

- Fluctuation  
- Dryness  
- Night glare  
- Subtle discomfort  

---

## Why this happens

Possible reasons include:
- Tear film instability  
- Healing variations  
- Optical changes  

---

## Why tests may still be “normal”

Vision charts measure clarity.

They do not capture:
👉 quality of vision

---

## The core idea

**Perfect numbers do not always mean perfect visual experience.**
