# Why Do I See Glare and Halos Around Lights?

Seeing halos or glare can be unsettling.

---

## Common causes

- Dry eye  
- Early cataract  
- Post-LASIK changes  
- Tear film instability  

---

## When it becomes noticeable

- Night driving  
- Low-light conditions  
- Screen exposure  

---

## Why tests may be normal

Routine charts do not capture:
👉 contrast and glare sensitivity  

---

## The core idea

**Glare is about quality of vision—not just clarity.**
