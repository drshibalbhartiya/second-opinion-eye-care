# Double Vision: When Should You Worry?

Seeing double is not something to ignore.

Even if it comes and goes.

---

## What double vision can mean

It may be related to:
- Eye muscle imbalance  
- Nerve problems  
- Systemic conditions  

---

## When it is urgent

- Sudden onset  
- Associated headache  
- Drooping eyelid  
- Weakness or imbalance  

---

## Why it is often delayed

Patients may:
- Adjust  
- Close one eye  
- Wait for it to pass  

---

## The core idea

**Double vision is a symptom, not a diagnosis—and it deserves evaluation.**
