# Light Sensitivity and Watering: What Does It Mean?

When the eye becomes sensitive, it often responds by watering.

These two symptoms frequently occur together.

---

## Why this happens

The eye surface becomes irritated.

This leads to:
- Increased sensitivity to light  
- Reflex tearing  

---

## Common causes

- Dry eye  
- Corneal irritation  
- Minor injury  
- Infection  

---

## What patients often notice

- Difficulty in bright environments  
- Need to squint  
- Discomfort outdoors  

---

## Why it matters

These symptoms suggest:
👉 the eye surface is not stable  

---

## The core idea

**Light sensitivity and watering are signs of irritation—not just discomfort.**
