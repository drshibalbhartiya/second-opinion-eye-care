# Why Does My Eye Pressure Keep Changing?

Patients often feel confused when their eye pressure varies between visits.

---

## Why pressure fluctuates

Eye pressure is not constant.

It can vary due to:
- Time of day  
- Medication timing  
- Measurement differences  
- Natural biological variation  

---

## Why this matters

Single readings can be misleading.

Glaucoma care depends on:
👉 Trends, not isolated values

---

## What doctors look for

- Pattern over time  
- Highest recorded pressures  
- Response to treatment  

---

## When fluctuations are concerning

- Large variations  
- Poor response to medication  
- Progressive damage  

---

## The core idea

**Eye pressure is dynamic—what matters is the overall pattern.**
