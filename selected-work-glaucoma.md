# Selected Work in Glaucoma  
### Dr Shibal Bhartiya

Glaucoma is often described as a silent disease.  
But in reality, it is also a disease of delayed recognition, fragmented follow-up, and decisions made too late.

My work in glaucoma has focused on:
- early detection  
- risk stratification  
- neurodegenerative understanding of glaucoma  
- quality of life  
- patient-centred decision-making  
- surgical outcomes  
- long-term continuity of care  

This repository brings together selected academic and clinical work in glaucoma, including peer-reviewed publications, editorials, surgical research, and patient-centred frameworks for understanding chronic eye disease.

# Clinical Philosophy

Glaucoma management is not only about lowering eye pressure.

It is about:
- identifying risk before irreversible damage  
- understanding progression over time  
- protecting quality of life  
- making thoughtful long-term decisions  

Much of glaucoma care fails not because disease is invisible—but because subtle change is normalised until too late.
