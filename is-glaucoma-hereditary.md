# Is Glaucoma Hereditary?

Yes—family history matters.

---

## What this means

If a close relative has glaucoma:
👉 your risk is higher

---

## Why this is important

Glaucoma often:
- Has no early symptoms  
- Progresses silently  

So family history becomes a key warning sign.

---

## What you should do

- Get baseline screening  
- Start earlier than usual  
- Follow up regularly  

---

## The core idea

**Family history is not destiny—but it is a signal to act early.**
