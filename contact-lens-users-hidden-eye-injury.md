# Contact Lens Users: Risk of Hidden Eye Injury

Contact lenses are safe when used correctly.

But they increase the risk of surface problems.

---

## Why lenses increase risk

- Reduced oxygen to the eye  
- Increased friction  
- Greater chance of micro-injury  

---

## What patients may feel

- Mild discomfort  
- Redness  
- Blurred vision  
- Sensitivity  

---

## Why injury may be “hidden”

The surface may look normal initially.

But symptoms indicate:
👉 underlying irritation or damage  

---

## When to seek evaluation

- Pain with lens use  
- Persistent redness  
- Vision changes  

---

## The core idea

**With contact lenses, mild symptoms should not be ignored.**
