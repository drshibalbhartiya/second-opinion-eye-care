# How Do You Know If Dry Eye Treatment Is Working?

Dry eye treatment can feel slow.

Many patients wonder:
👉 “Is this actually helping?”

---

## What improvement looks like

- Less burning  
- More stable vision  
- Reduced fluctuation  
- Better comfort during the day  

---

## What may not change immediately

- Complete symptom resolution  
- Occasional dryness  

---

## Why patience is needed

Dry eye is:
👉 chronic  

Treatment works:
👉 gradually  

---

## What to track

- Frequency of symptoms  
- Triggers  
- Daily comfort  

---

## The core idea

**Dry eye improvement is gradual—not instant.**
