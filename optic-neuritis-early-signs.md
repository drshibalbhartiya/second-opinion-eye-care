# Optic Neuritis: Early Signs Patients Should Know

Optic neuritis affects the optic nerve.

---

## Early symptoms

- Pain with eye movement  
- Sudden vision reduction  
- Reduced colour vision  

---

## Why it matters

It may be linked to:
👉 neurological conditions  

---

## When to seek help

- Sudden visual change  
- Eye pain with vision issues  

---

## The core idea

**Painful vision loss is not typical—it needs evaluation.**
