# 5 Early Glaucoma Symptoms Most People Ignore

Glaucoma is often called the “silent thief of sight.”

But the truth is—it’s not always completely silent.

The early signs are subtle.  
Easy to dismiss.  
And often missed until damage is advanced.

---

## Why early glaucoma is missed

In the early stages:
- Vision may still be 6/6 or 20/20  
- There is no pain  
- Routine tests may appear normal  

This creates a dangerous illusion:
👉 “Everything is fine.”

---

## 1. Difficulty seeing in low light

You may notice:
- Struggling in dim rooms  
- Difficulty adjusting to darkness  
- Reduced comfort while driving at night  

This is often ignored as “normal ageing.”

---

## 2. Increased glare and halos

Lights may feel:
- Too bright  
- Surrounded by halos  
- Uncomfortable at night  

Patients often attribute this to glasses or screen use.

---

## 3. Subtle peripheral vision changes

Early glaucoma affects:
👉 Side vision first  

But because central vision is intact:
- You don’t notice immediately  
- The brain compensates  

---

## 4. Eye strain despite clear vision

You may:
- See clearly on tests  
- Still feel visual fatigue  
- Experience discomfort during prolonged tasks  

This mismatch is a key early signal.

---

## 5. A vague sense that something is “off”

Many patients say:

> “I can see, but something doesn’t feel right.”

This is one of the most important—and most ignored—early warnings.

---

## Who is at higher risk?

- Family history of glaucoma  
- Age over 40  
- High eye pressure (IOP)  
- Diabetes or vascular risk factors  

---

## Why early detection matters

Glaucoma damage is:
👉 **Irreversible**

But if detected early:
👉 Progression can be slowed or stopped

---

## When to seek a second opinion

Consider a detailed evaluation if:
- You have symptoms but normal reports  
- You are in a high-risk group  
- Your visual experience does not match your test results  

---

## The core idea

**You can have perfect vision—and still have early glaucoma.**

## FAQs

### Can glaucoma be detected early?
Yes, but it often requires specific tests beyond routine eye exams.

---

### If my vision is normal, can I still have glaucoma?
Yes. Early glaucoma does not affect central vision.

---

### Do I need treatment if I feel fine?
Sometimes yes, because treatment prevents future damage.

---

### Is glaucoma always due to high pressure?
No. Some patients develop glaucoma even with normal pressure.
