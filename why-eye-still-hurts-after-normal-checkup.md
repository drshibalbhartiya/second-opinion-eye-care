# Why Does My Eye Still Hurt After a “Normal” Checkup?

This is a common and frustrating situation.

You’ve been told everything is normal—but the discomfort continues.

---

## What “normal” actually means

It usually means:
- No structural damage  
- No visible disease  

But it does not always explain:
👉 how the eye feels  

---

## Possible reasons

- Dry eye  
- Surface irritation  
- Healing after minor injury  
- Nerve sensitivity  

---

## Why symptoms persist

Some conditions affect:
👉 function, not structure  

These are harder to “see” in routine exams.

---

## What helps

- Tracking symptoms over time  
- Re-evaluating if symptoms persist  
- Looking beyond basic findings  

---

## The core idea

**A normal report does not always mean a normal experience.**
