# Am I a Good Candidate for LASIK?

Many patients assume LASIK is suitable for everyone.

But not all eyes are the same.

---

## What LASIK does

LASIK reshapes the cornea to:
👉 reduce dependence on glasses  

---

## What determines suitability

- Corneal thickness and shape  
- Stability of your prescription  
- Overall eye health  
- Presence of dry eye  

---

## When LASIK may not be ideal

- Thin or irregular cornea  
- Unstable number  
- Significant dry eye  
- Certain underlying conditions  

---

## Why proper evaluation matters

A quick screening is not enough.

Careful assessment helps:
👉 avoid long-term issues  

---

## The core idea

**LASIK is not about eligibility—it’s about suitability.**
