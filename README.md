## Why this exists

Many patients are told their eyes are “normal” because they can see 6/6 or 20/20 on a chart.

But real-world vision is more complex.

- You may see clearly—and still struggle at night  
- Your reports may look fine—and something still feels off  
- Early disease may not show up in routine testing  

This gap between **clinical results and lived experience** is where problems are often missed.
