# When Is Glaucoma Surgery Needed?

Most patients with glaucoma start with eye drops.

But sometimes, treatment needs to move beyond drops.

---

## When surgery is considered

Surgery may be advised if:

- Eye pressure is not controlled despite medication  
- There is progression on scans or visual fields  
- Drops are not tolerated or difficult to use regularly  

---

## Why timing matters

Delaying surgery too long can:
👉 allow further irreversible damage  

Doing it too early may:
👉 expose you to unnecessary intervention  

---

## What your doctor is balancing

- Current stage of glaucoma  
- Rate of progression  
- Target pressure required  
- Response to medication  

---

## The core idea

**Glaucoma surgery is not a last resort—it is a step in long-term protection when needed.**
