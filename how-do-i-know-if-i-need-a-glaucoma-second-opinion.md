# How Do I Know If I Need a Glaucoma Second Opinion?

Not every patient needs one.

But some situations benefit from clarity.

---

## When a second opinion helps

- Conflicting advice  
- Unclear diagnosis  
- Multiple medications without explanation  
- Persistent doubt  

---

## What it provides

- Independent assessment  
- Clarity on risk  
- Confidence in your plan  

---

## What it is not

👉 It is not about mistrust  
👉 It is about informed decision-making  

---

## The core idea

**A second opinion is about clarity—not contradiction.**
