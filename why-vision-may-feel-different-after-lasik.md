# Why Does Vision Feel Different After LASIK?

Some patients say:

“I can see clearly—but it doesn’t feel the same.”

---

## What patients notice

- Fluctuation  
- Dryness  
- Glare or halos  
- Visual fatigue  

---

## Why this happens

- Tear film changes  
- Healing process  
- Optical adaptation  

---

## Why tests may look normal

Vision charts measure clarity.

But real-world vision includes:
👉 comfort and stability  

---

## When to follow up

- Persistent discomfort  
- Difficulty with night vision  
- Ongoing fluctuation  

---

## The core idea

**Clear vision and comfortable vision are not always the same.**
