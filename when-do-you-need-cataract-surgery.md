# When Do You Actually Need Cataract Surgery?

Many patients are told:

“You have cataract.”

But the real question is:
👉 Do you need surgery now?

---

## What cataract means

A cataract is:
👉 clouding of the natural lens  

It develops gradually.

---

## When surgery is needed

Not based on the presence of cataract alone.

But when it begins to affect:
- Daily activities (reading, driving)  
- Quality of vision  
- Comfort in real-world situations  

---

## When you may not need it yet

- Vision is still comfortable  
- No significant functional difficulty  
- Symptoms are mild  

---

## What matters more than numbers

Vision tests may look acceptable.

But decision depends on:
👉 how you experience your vision  

---

## The core idea

**Cataract surgery is based on need—not just diagnosis.**
