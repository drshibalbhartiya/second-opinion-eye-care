# Can You Have Glaucoma With Normal Eye Pressure?

Yes—you can.

This surprises many patients.

---

## What most people believe

High eye pressure = glaucoma  
Normal pressure = safe  

This is not always true.

---

## What is normal-tension glaucoma?

In some patients:
- Eye pressure is within “normal range”  
- But optic nerve damage still occurs  

---

## Why this happens

Factors may include:
- Reduced blood flow  
- Optic nerve susceptibility  
- Systemic conditions  

---

## Why this is often missed

Because:
- Pressure appears normal  
- Symptoms are absent  
- Routine exams may not detect damage  

---

## What matters more than pressure

- Optic nerve health  
- Visual field changes  
- OCT findings  

---

## The core idea

**Normal pressure does not always mean no glaucoma.**
