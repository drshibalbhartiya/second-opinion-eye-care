# Why Does My Vision Fluctuate Throughout the Day?

Many patients notice:

- Vision is clear in the morning  
- Blurry or uncomfortable later  
- Worse with screens  

And yet, eye tests appear normal.

---

## The common cause: Dry eye

Dry eye is not just irritation.

It affects:
👉 **how stable your vision is**

---

## Why vision fluctuates

A healthy tear film creates a smooth optical surface.

When it is unstable:
- Vision becomes inconsistent  
- Clarity comes and goes  
- Blinking temporarily improves vision  

---

## Typical pattern

- Better after rest  
- Worse with screen use  
- Fluctuates during the day  

---

## Why this is often missed

Routine tests may still show:
- Normal vision  
- Normal eye structure  

---

## The core idea

**Stable vision depends on tear film—not just the eye itself.**
