# Is My Glaucoma Being Monitored—or Just Delayed?

Many patients are told:

“Let’s wait and watch.”

This can be appropriate—but not always.

---

## What proper monitoring means

Monitoring is active.

It involves:
- Regular testing  
- Comparing results over time  
- Adjusting treatment when needed  

---

## When monitoring becomes delay

- No clear follow-up plan  
- Repeated reassurance without explanation  
- Progression noticed late  

---

## Why this matters

Glaucoma damage is:
👉 irreversible  

Delay can mean:
👉 missed opportunity to protect vision  

---

## The core idea

**Monitoring should be active—not passive reassurance.**
