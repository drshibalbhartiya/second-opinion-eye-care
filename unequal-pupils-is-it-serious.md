# Unequal Pupils (Anisocoria): Should You Be Concerned?

Noticing that one pupil is larger than the other can be unsettling.

---

## What is anisocoria?

👉 A difference in pupil size between the two eyes  

---

## When it is harmless

- Long-standing, unchanged difference  
- No associated symptoms  

---

## When to pay attention

- Sudden onset  
- Associated drooping eyelid  
- Double vision  
- Headache or pain  

---

## Why this matters

In some cases, it may indicate:
👉 nerve-related issues  

---

## The core idea

**Not all asymmetry is dangerous—but sudden change should be evaluated.**
