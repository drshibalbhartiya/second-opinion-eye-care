# When Should You Go to Emergency for an Eye Problem?

Not every eye problem needs urgent care.

But some should not be delayed.

---

## Situations that need immediate attention

- Sudden loss of vision  
- Severe eye pain  
- Chemical exposure  
- Injury to the eye  

---

## Situations that may wait briefly

- Mild irritation  
- Dryness  
- Gradual discomfort  

---

## Why this distinction matters

Unnecessary panic leads to stress.

Delayed action can lead to:
👉 preventable damage  

---

## What patients often struggle with

- Underestimating serious symptoms  
- Overreacting to minor ones  

---

## The core idea

**Urgency is determined by pattern, not just intensity.**
