# Why Do My Eyes Feel Sensitive to Light?

Light sensitivity (photophobia) can occur even when vision seems normal.

---

## Possible causes

- Dry eye  
- Surface irritation  
- Migraine  
- Neurological factors  

---

## What patients feel

- Discomfort in bright light  
- Need to squint  
- Eye fatigue outdoors  

---

## Why this matters

It may reflect:
👉 surface or neurological sensitivity  

---

## When to evaluate

- Persistent discomfort  
- Associated pain  
- Vision changes  

---

## The core idea

**Light sensitivity is a symptom—not a diagnosis.**
