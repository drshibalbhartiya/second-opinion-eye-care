# When Are Headaches Related to Eye Problems?

Not all headaches are due to the eyes.

But sometimes, the connection is important.

---

## Common overlaps

- Eye strain causing headache  
- Visual issues triggering discomfort  
- Neurological causes affecting both  

---

## When to pay attention

- Persistent headaches with visual symptoms  
- Double vision  
- Sudden changes in vision  

---

## Why this gets confusing

Patients may:
- Assume all headaches are eye-related  
- Or ignore eye involvement completely  

---

## The core idea

**Headache and vision can overlap—but need careful evaluation.**
