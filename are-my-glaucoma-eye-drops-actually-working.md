# Are My Glaucoma Eye Drops Actually Working?

Many patients use drops regularly—but still feel unsure.

---

## What drops are meant to do

- Lower eye pressure  
- Slow progression  
- Protect the optic nerve  

---

## How you know they are working

- Pressure is within target  
- No progression on tests  
- Stable reports over time  

---

## When to question effectiveness

- Pressure fluctuates widely  
- Reports show change  
- Increasing number of medications  

---

## The core idea

**Effectiveness is judged over time—not a single reading.**
