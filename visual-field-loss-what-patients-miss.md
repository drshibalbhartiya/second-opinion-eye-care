# Visual Field Loss: Why Patients Often Miss It

Visual field loss does not always feel obvious.

---

## What it means

Loss of:
👉 side (peripheral) vision  

---

## Why it is missed

- Central vision remains clear  
- The brain compensates  
- Changes are gradual  

---

## Where it matters

Seen in:
- Glaucoma  
- Neurological conditions  

---

## What patients notice

Often:
👉 nothing—until advanced  

---

## The core idea

**Vision can feel normal even when parts of it are missing.**
