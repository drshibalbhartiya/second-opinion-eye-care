# Why Do My Eyes Water If They Are Dry?

It sounds contradictory:

“My eyes feel dry—but they keep watering.”

---

## Why this happens

Dryness irritates the eye surface.

This triggers:
👉 reflex tearing  

---

## The problem

These tears:
- Are excessive  
- But not stable  
- Do not lubricate properly  

---

## What you experience

- Watering outdoors  
- Watering in wind  
- Blurry vision with tearing  

---

## Why it is misunderstood

Patients assume:
👉 watering = no dryness  

But the opposite can be true.

---

## The core idea

**Watering can be a sign of dryness—not the absence of it.**
