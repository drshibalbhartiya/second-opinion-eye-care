# Why Do My Eyes Burn When I Use Screens?

This is one of the most common modern complaints.

---

## What happens during screen use

- Blink rate reduces  
- Tear film evaporates faster  
- Surface becomes unstable  

---

## What you feel

- Burning  
- Dryness  
- Irritation  
- Fatigue  

---

## Why tests may still be normal

Because:
👉 structure is normal  
👉 function is affected  

---

## What helps

- Regular breaks  
- Conscious blinking  
- Treating underlying dry eye  

---

## The core idea

**Screen strain is often a tear film problem—not just overuse.**
