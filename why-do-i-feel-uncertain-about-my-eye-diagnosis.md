# Why Do I Feel Uncertain About My Eye Diagnosis?

Many patients leave consultations feeling:

“I’ve been told I’m fine—but I’m not convinced.”

---

## Why this happens

- Early disease is subtle  
- Tests may not be definitive  
- Explanations may feel incomplete  

---

## What uncertainty means

It does not always mean something is wrong.

But it does mean:
👉 you need clarity  

---

## What helps

- Asking specific questions  
- Reviewing reports over time  
- Seeking a second opinion if needed  

---

## The core idea

**Uncertainty is a signal to understand—not to ignore.**
