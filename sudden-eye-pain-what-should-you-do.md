# Sudden Eye Pain: What Should You Do?

Sudden eye pain can be unsettling.

Sometimes it is minor and settles on its own.  
Sometimes it is a signal that needs attention.

The key is not to panic—but not to ignore it either.

---

## What can cause sudden eye pain?

Eye pain can arise from different parts of the eye:

- The surface (dry eye, irritation, scratch)  
- The inside of the eye (pressure-related problems)  
- Surrounding structures (eyelids, muscles)  

This is why the **type of pain matters more than the intensity alone**.

---

## What you should do first

If you develop sudden eye pain:

- Avoid rubbing the eye  
- Do not use random drops  
- Notice if there is redness, watering, or vision change  

These details help determine whether it is minor or significant.

---

## When it is likely to be mild

Pain may settle with simple care if:

- It started after dust exposure  
- There is mild irritation without vision change  
- It improves with blinking or washing  

---

## When you should not wait

Seek evaluation if you have:

- Severe or persistent pain  
- Blurred or reduced vision  
- Light sensitivity  
- Redness with discomfort  

---

## Why this matters

The eye does not have many ways to signal distress.

Pain is one of the few.

---

## The core idea

**Eye pain is not a diagnosis—it is a signal.  
What matters is understanding its cause in time.**
