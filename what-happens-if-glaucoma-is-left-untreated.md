# What Happens If Glaucoma Is Left Untreated?

Because glaucoma is often silent, it is sometimes underestimated.

---

## What untreated glaucoma does

- Gradual optic nerve damage  
- Progressive loss of peripheral vision  
- Eventual impact on central vision  

---

## Why patients delay

- Vision feels normal  
- No pain  
- Reports may seem unclear  

---

## Why early treatment matters

Damage in glaucoma is:
👉 irreversible  

But progression can often be:
👉 slowed or stopped  

---

## The core idea

**Glaucoma does not cause early symptoms—but it does cause permanent loss if ignored.**
