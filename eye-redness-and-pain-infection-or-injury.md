# Eye Redness + Pain: Infection or Injury?

Redness in the eye is common.

Redness with pain is different.

---

## Why pain changes the picture

Redness alone may be due to:
- Dry eye  
- Allergy  
- Mild irritation  

But when pain is present:
👉 it suggests deeper involvement  

---

## Possible causes

- Surface injury (scratch, foreign body)  
- Infection  
- Inflammation  

---

## What helps differentiate

- Sudden onset after exposure → often injury  
- Gradual onset with discharge → often infection  
- Light sensitivity → surface or deeper involvement  

---

## What patients often miss

They focus on redness.

But clinically:
👉 pain is the more important signal  

---

## When to seek evaluation

- Persistent discomfort  
- Vision affected  
- Sensitivity to light  

---

## The core idea

**Redness is common. Pain gives it meaning.**
