# What Does Continuity Protect?

Continuity is often treated as convenience.

But continuity may itself be treatment.

Longitudinal care allows:

- recognition of subtle change
- understanding of baseline variability
- calibration of risk tolerance
- better interpretation of tests over time
- patient confidence in decision-making

Many important clinical decisions emerge not from one test or one visit—but from accumulated context.

The question is not simply:

“Who saw the patient today?”

But:

“Who remembers who this patient was two years ago?”
