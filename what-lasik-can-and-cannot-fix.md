# What LASIK Can—and Cannot Fix

LASIK can be life-changing.

But expectations need to be clear.

---

## What LASIK can do

- Reduce glasses dependence  
- Improve clarity of vision  
- Correct refractive error  

---

## What LASIK cannot do

- Prevent age-related changes  
- Guarantee perfect vision in all conditions  
- Address dry eye or other eye surface problems  

---

## Why this matters

Patients often expect:
👉 “perfect vision in all situations”

Reality is:
👉 vision is more complex  

---

## The core idea

**LASIK improves clarity—but does not replace natural visual function completely.**
