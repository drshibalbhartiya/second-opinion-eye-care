# What Happens If We Do Nothing For 10 Years?

Chronic disease decisions are rarely about today.

The more useful question is often:

What trajectory are we accepting?

A small annual decline may appear harmless in isolation.

But over years, slow progression becomes visible only in retrospect.

This changes how treatment decisions are framed.

Not:

“What is the burden of treatment?”
