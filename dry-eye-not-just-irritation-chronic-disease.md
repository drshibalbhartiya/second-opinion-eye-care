# Dry Eye Is Not Just Irritation—It Is a Chronic Condition

Many people think dry eye means:

- Occasional burning  
- Mild redness  
- Temporary discomfort  

But in many patients, it is a long-term condition.

---

## Why dry eye is underestimated

Symptoms may seem minor.

But over time:
- Vision quality drops  
- Comfort reduces  
- Daily functioning is affected  

---

## The mismatch

Often:
- Symptoms are severe  
- Tests appear mild  

This leads to:
👉 under-treatment or dismissal

---

## What actually drives dry eye

- Meibomian gland dysfunction  
- Screen use  
- Hormonal factors  
- Environment  

---

## The core idea

**Dry eye is a chronic condition that needs ongoing care—not occasional drops.**
