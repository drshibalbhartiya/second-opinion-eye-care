# Drooping Eyelid (Ptosis): What Does It Mean?

A drooping eyelid can be:
- Subtle  
- Gradual  
- Or suddenly noticeable  

---

## Possible causes

- Age-related changes  
- Muscle weakness  
- Nerve-related conditions  

---

## When it is concerning

- Sudden onset  
- Associated double vision  
- Difficulty keeping the eye open  

---

## Why evaluation matters

The cause determines:
👉 whether it is simple or serious  

---

## The core idea

**A drooping eyelid is a sign—not a diagnosis.**
