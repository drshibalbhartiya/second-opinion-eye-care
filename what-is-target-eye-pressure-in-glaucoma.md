# What Is “Target Eye Pressure” in Glaucoma?

Patients often hear:

“Your pressure needs to be lower.”

But lower to what?

---

## What is target pressure?

Target eye pressure is:
👉 the level at which further damage is unlikely

---

## Why it is different for each person

There is no single “safe number.”

It depends on:
- Stage of glaucoma  
- Rate of progression  
- Optic nerve health  

---

## Why this matters

Two patients with the same pressure may:
- Have different risks  
- Need different treatment  

---

## How it is used

Doctors adjust treatment to:
👉 reach and maintain this target

---

## The core idea

**In glaucoma, the right pressure is a personalised goal—not a fixed number.**
