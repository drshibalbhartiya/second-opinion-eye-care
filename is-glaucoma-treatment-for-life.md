# Is Glaucoma Treatment for Life?

This is one of the most common—and difficult—questions.

The short answer:
👉 Usually, yes.

---

## Why treatment is long-term

Glaucoma:
- Is chronic  
- Cannot be reversed  
- Can only be controlled  

---

## What treatment aims to do

- Lower eye pressure  
- Prevent further damage  
- Preserve vision  

---

## Can treatment ever stop?

Rarely.

But it may:
- Change  
- Reduce  
- Shift to other options  

---

## Alternatives to drops

In some cases:
- Laser treatment  
- Surgery  

These may reduce dependence on drops.

---

## The core idea

**Glaucoma treatment is about long-term protection, not short-term cure.**
