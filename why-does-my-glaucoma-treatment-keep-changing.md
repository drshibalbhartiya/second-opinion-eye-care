# Why Does My Glaucoma Treatment Keep Changing?

Patients often feel unsettled when treatment changes.

---

## Why treatment evolves

Glaucoma is dynamic.

Changes may reflect:
- New findings  
- Progression  
- Response to therapy  

---

## What this does NOT mean

It does not always mean:
👉 things are getting worse  

---

## What it usually means

👉 treatment is being adjusted to protect vision  

---

## The core idea

**Changing treatment often reflects active care—not instability.**
