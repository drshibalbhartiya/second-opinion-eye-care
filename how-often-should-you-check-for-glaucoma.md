# How Often Should You Get Checked for Glaucoma?

There isn’t a single answer for everyone.

The right frequency depends on your risk.

---

## If you have no risk factors

- Baseline check around age 40  
- Periodic screening as advised  

---

## If you have risk factors

- Family history  
- High eye pressure  
- Diabetes or vascular conditions  

👉 More frequent checks are needed

---

## If you already have glaucoma

Follow-up is critical:

- Regular pressure monitoring  
- Periodic visual fields  
- OCT scans  

---

## Why frequency matters

Glaucoma changes over time.

Missing follow-ups means:
👉 missing progression

---

## The core idea

**Glaucoma care is continuous, not one-time.**
