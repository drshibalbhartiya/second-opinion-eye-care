# Why Is My Vision Not Perfect After Cataract Surgery?

Many patients expect perfect vision after surgery.

Most improve significantly—but some notice:

👉 something still feels off  

---

## Why this happens

Possible reasons include:
- Dry eye  
- Residual refractive error  
- Adaptation to new optics  
- Other underlying eye conditions  

---

## Why tests may still look good

Vision charts measure clarity.

But real-world vision includes:
👉 comfort, contrast, and stability  

---

## What patients often feel

- Slight blur  
- Glare or halos  
- Fluctuation  

---

## What to do

- Give time for adaptation  
- Follow up if symptoms persist  
- Evaluate for other causes  

---

## The core idea

**Good surgery does not always mean perfect visual experience.**
