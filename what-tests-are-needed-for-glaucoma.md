# What Tests Are Needed for Glaucoma?

Many patients ask:

“Which tests actually matter?”

---

## Core tests for glaucoma

A proper evaluation includes:

### 1. Eye pressure (IOP)
Measures internal pressure

---

### 2. Optic nerve examination
Assesses structural damage

---

### 3. Visual field test
Detects functional vision loss

---

### 4. OCT scan
Shows early nerve damage

---

## Why multiple tests are needed

No single test is enough.

Glaucoma is diagnosed by:
👉 combining findings

---

## The core idea

**Glaucoma is a pattern—not a single test result.**
