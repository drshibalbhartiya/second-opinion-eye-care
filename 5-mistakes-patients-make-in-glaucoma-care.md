# 5 Mistakes Patients Make in Glaucoma Care

Glaucoma is not difficult because of complexity alone.

It is difficult because small mistakes, repeated over time, cause damage.

---

## 1. Relying only on vision clarity

Seeing clearly does not mean disease is controlled.

---

## 2. Skipping follow-ups

Glaucoma progression is silent.

Missing visits means missing change.

---

## 3. Irregular use of eye drops

Even occasional missed doses can:
👉 Affect pressure control

---

## 4. Not understanding the treatment plan

Many patients take drops without knowing:
- Why  
- For how long  
- What the goal is  

---

## 5. Ignoring subtle symptoms

Early warning signs are often dismissed.

---

## The core idea

**Consistency matters more than intensity in glaucoma care.**
