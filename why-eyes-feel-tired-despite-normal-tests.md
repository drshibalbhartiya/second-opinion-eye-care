# Why Do My Eyes Feel Tired Even When Tests Are Normal?

Many patients say:

“My reports are normal, but my eyes feel tired all the time.”

---

## Why this happens

Fatigue is often due to:
- Dry eye  
- Screen strain  
- Tear film instability  

---

## Why tests may look normal

Routine exams focus on:
- Vision clarity  
- Eye structure  

They may not capture:
👉 functional discomfort

---

## Common triggers

- Long screen hours  
- Reduced blinking  
- Environmental factors  

---

## The core idea

**Comfort and clarity are not the same thing.**
