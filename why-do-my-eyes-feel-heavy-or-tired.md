# Why Do My Eyes Feel Heavy or Tired?

This is one of the most common complaints.

---

## What patients describe

- Heaviness  
- Fatigue  
- Discomfort  
- Need to rest eyes frequently  

---

## Common causes

- Dry eye  
- Screen strain  
- Reduced blinking  
- Poor tear quality  

---

## Why this matters

Even when vision is “normal”:
👉 comfort may not be  

---

## What it affects

- Work productivity  
- Reading  
- Screen tolerance  

---

## The core idea

**Eye fatigue is often a surface problem—not a vision problem.**
