# Glaucoma vs Eye Pressure: What Matters More?

Many patients equate glaucoma with high eye pressure.

But the relationship is more nuanced.

---

## The common assumption

High pressure = glaucoma  
Normal pressure = safe  

This is not always true.

---

## What actually matters

Glaucoma is defined by:
👉 **optic nerve damage and visual field changes**

Eye pressure is:
👉 a major risk factor—but not the whole story

---

## Two important realities

- Some patients have high pressure without damage (ocular hypertension)  
- Some develop glaucoma with normal pressure (normal-tension glaucoma)

---

## What your doctor is really assessing

- Optic nerve structure (OCT)  
- Functional loss (visual fields)  
- Pressure trends over time  
- Individual susceptibility  

---

## The core idea

**Treat the patient, not just the pressure number.**
