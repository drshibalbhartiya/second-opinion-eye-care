# Glaucoma Surgery: What Should You Expect?

The idea of surgery can feel overwhelming.

Understanding the process helps reduce anxiety.

---

## Before surgery

- Detailed evaluation  
- Planning based on your condition  
- Discussion of risks and benefits  

---

## During surgery

- Usually done under local anaesthesia  
- Focus is on improving fluid drainage  

---

## After surgery

- Follow-up is essential  
- Medications may still be needed  
- Healing varies between patients  

---

## What surgery aims to do

👉 Lower eye pressure  
👉 Protect remaining vision  

---

## The core idea

**Glaucoma surgery is about preserving vision—not restoring lost sight.**
