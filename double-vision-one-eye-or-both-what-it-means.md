# Double Vision: Is It in One Eye or Both—and Why It Matters

Double vision can feel alarming.

But an important first question is:
👉 Does it happen in one eye or both?

---

## If it happens in one eye (monocular)

- Often related to the eye itself  
- Common causes include:
  - Dry eye  
  - Corneal irregularity  
  - Cataract  

---

## If it happens in both eyes (binocular)

- Usually due to alignment issues  
- May involve:
  - Eye muscle imbalance  
  - Nerve-related problems  

---

## Why this distinction matters

It helps determine:
👉 whether the cause is local or neurological  

---

## When to seek evaluation

- Sudden onset  
- Persistent double vision  
- Associated symptoms  

---

## The core idea

**Not all double vision is the same—the pattern matters.**
