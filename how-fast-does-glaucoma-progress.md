# How Fast Does Glaucoma Progress?

One of the most common fears patients have is:

“Will I lose vision quickly?”

The answer is not simple—but it is important.

---

## Glaucoma progression varies

Glaucoma does not behave the same in everyone.

It can be:
- Very slow  
- Moderately progressive  
- Occasionally aggressive  

---

## What determines progression

Several factors influence speed:

- Eye pressure level  
- Type of glaucoma  
- Stage at diagnosis  
- Individual susceptibility  

---

## The role of early detection

If caught early:
👉 Progression can often be slowed significantly  

If detected late:
👉 Damage may already be advanced  

---

## Why regular follow-up matters

Glaucoma is not a one-time diagnosis.

It requires:
- Monitoring over time  
- Repeat testing  
- Treatment adjustments  

---

## Signs of progression

Often, there are no obvious symptoms.

Progression is usually detected through:
- Visual field changes  
- Optic nerve imaging  

---

## What patients often misunderstand

- “My vision is fine, so it’s not progressing” ❌  
- “I don’t feel anything, so it’s stable” ❌  

Glaucoma does not announce itself.

---

## When to worry

- Increasing eye pressure  
- Worsening test results  
- Poor adherence to treatment  

---

## The core idea

**Glaucoma progression is unpredictable—but manageable with timely care.**
