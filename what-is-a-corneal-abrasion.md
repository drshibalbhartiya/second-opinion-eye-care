# What Is a Corneal Abrasion (Eye Scratch)?

A corneal abrasion is a scratch on the clear front surface of the eye.

Even a very small scratch can feel disproportionately painful.

---

## Why it feels so uncomfortable

The cornea is one of the most sensitive tissues in the body.

So even a minor injury can cause:
- Sharp pain  
- Watering  
- Light sensitivity  
- Difficulty keeping the eye open  

---

## Common causes

- Fingernail injury  
- Dust or foreign body  
- Rubbing the eye  
- Contact lens use  

---

## What patients often notice

The discomfort is usually immediate.

Some describe it as:
👉 “something stuck in the eye that won’t go away”

---

## What matters clinically

The size of the scratch matters less than:
👉 how the surface heals  
👉 whether infection develops  

---

## When to seek evaluation

- Persistent pain  
- Blurred vision  
- Light sensitivity  
- Symptoms not improving  

---

## The core idea

**A small scratch can feel severe—but with proper care, it usually heals well.**
