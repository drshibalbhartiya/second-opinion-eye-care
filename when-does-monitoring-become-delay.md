# When Does Monitoring Become Delay?

Monitoring is essential.

But monitoring without a clear decision framework can quietly become postponement.

Many chronic eye diseases progress slowly, unevenly, and often invisibly.

The question is rarely:

“Has this changed?”

More often it is:

“If this trend continues, what will this patient lose over the next decade?”

Monitoring should reduce uncertainty.

Not transfer responsibility to time.

A recurring clinical challenge is identifying the point where observation stops protecting patients and starts protecting comfort.
