# When Should an Eye Problem Actually Worry You?

Not every symptom is serious.

But some deserve attention.

---

## Common confusion

Patients often:
- Ignore important symptoms  
- Worry about minor ones  

---

## When to pay attention

- Sudden vision change  
- Persistent discomfort  
- Symptoms not matching reports  
- Risk factors present  

---

## What matters most

- Change over time  
- Persistence  
- Context  

---

## The core idea

**It’s not just the symptom—it’s the pattern that matters.**
