# How Do You Know If Glaucoma Is Worsening?

Glaucoma does not usually “feel” worse.

---

## Why progression is hard to detect

- No early symptoms  
- Vision may remain clear  
- Changes are gradual  

---

## How doctors detect worsening

- Visual field comparison  
- OCT progression analysis  
- Eye pressure trends  

---

## What patients often assume

- “I feel fine, so it must be stable” ❌  

---

## What actually matters

👉 Change over time—not how you feel today  

---

## The core idea

**Glaucoma progression is detected on tests—not symptoms.**
