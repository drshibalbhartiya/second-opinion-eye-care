# What I Look for When I Review a Glaucoma Patient

A second opinion in glaucoma is not just about confirming a diagnosis.

It is about understanding risk, progression, and long-term safety.

---

## 1. Is this truly glaucoma?

Not all optic nerve changes mean glaucoma.

I first ask:
- Is there real damage?  
- Or is this a variation being overtreated?  

---

## 2. What is the stage?

Early, moderate, or advanced?

This determines:
- Urgency  
- Treatment intensity  
- Monitoring frequency  

---

## 3. What is the target pressure?

There is no “one normal number.”

Each patient has a:
👉 **personalised target pressure**

---

## 4. Are current treatments appropriate?

- Are the drops necessary?  
- Are they sufficient?  
- Can they be simplified?  

---

## 5. Is there progression?

I compare:
- Old reports  
- Visual fields  
- OCT scans  

Because glaucoma is about **change over time**

---

## 6. What is the long-term plan?

Not just:
👉 “What now?”

But:
👉 “What next over years?”

---

## The core idea

**Glaucoma care is not about a single visit.  
It is about a long-term strategy.**
