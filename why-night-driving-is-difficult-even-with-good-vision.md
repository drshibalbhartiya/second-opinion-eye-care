# Why Is Night Driving Difficult Even When My Vision Is “Normal”?

Many patients say:

“I can see clearly in clinic—but struggle at night.”

---

## Why this happens

Standard tests measure:
- High contrast vision  

But night driving involves:
- Low contrast  
- Glare  
- Dynamic lighting  

---

## Possible causes

- Early cataract  
- Dry eye  
- Glare sensitivity  
- Functional vision issues  

---

## Why tests may appear normal

Routine charts do not reflect:
👉 real-world visual demands

---

## The core idea

**Seeing well in clinic does not always translate to real-world comfort.**
