# Eye Pain with a Normal Eye Exam: What Does It Mean?

Some patients feel:

“My eye hurts—but I’ve been told everything is normal.”

---

## Why this happens

Pain does not always come from visible damage.

Possible causes include:
- Dry eye  
- Surface irritation  
- Neurological causes  
- Referred pain  

---

## Why tests may appear normal

Routine exams check:
- Structure  
- Vision  

But may not capture:
👉 functional or nerve-related discomfort  

---

## When to pay attention

- Persistent pain  
- Pain with vision changes  
- Associated headache  

---

## The core idea

**Normal tests do not always explain symptoms—context matters.**
